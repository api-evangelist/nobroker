RK Puram Metro Station is one of the key underground stations on the Delhi Metro network. Located on the Magenta Line, it provides convenient connectivity to important residential areas, educational institutions, business centres, and commercial hubs across Delhi and Noida. The station helps office-goers, students, and families travel while avoiding daily road traffic congestion.
