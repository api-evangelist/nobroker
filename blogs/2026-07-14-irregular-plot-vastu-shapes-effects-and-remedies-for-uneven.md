Irregular shaped plots like L-shaped, triangular or circular ones are considered a Vastu defect since they disrupt energy balance. Square or rectangular plots are ideal. Remedies include dividing the plot into regular sections with separate compound walls, placing mirrors or Vastu pyramids in missing corners, and using extended areas for gardens.
