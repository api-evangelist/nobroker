# NoBroker

> NoBroker Technologies Solutions Pvt Ltd is an Indian proptech company (founded 2013,
> Bengaluru) running a brokerage-free real estate marketplace that connects property
> owners directly with tenants and buyers, plus adjacent home services and the
> NoBrokerHood housing-society management / ERP product. NoBroker publishes no public
> developer program: there is no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract,
> no SDK and no MCP server. This file is generated by API Evangelist from the public
> web surface, not published by NoBroker.

## APIs

- No public API. NoBroker ships software only as an end-user web and mobile product.
  Its application endpoints under `/api/v1/` through `/api/v5/` are explicitly
  disallowed for crawlers in https://www.nobroker.in/robots.txt and are not documented.

## Products

- [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in): Brokerage-free marketplace for renting, buying
  and selling flats, houses, villas, plots, PGs, flatmates and commercial space in
  Indian metros.
- [NoBrokerHood](https://www.nobrokerhood.com): Gated-community and housing-society
  management platform and ERP — visitor management, billing and accounting, staff
  attendance, complaints and community payments.
- Home services: rental and lease agreement drafting, legal assistance, packers and
  movers, painting and cleaning, home loans, and rent payments.
